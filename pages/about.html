The Birthplace of Arabica

Coffee originates from the Kaffa region of Ethiopia, where wild Arabica trees still grow in the highland forests. Over centuries, preparing and sharing coffee became a cornerstone of Ethiopian hospitality.

Essential Ceremony Equipment

Hosting an authentic ceremony requires a few traditional tools:

  • Jebena: A round-bottomed black clay pot used to brew the coffee over hot coals.
  • Menkeshkesh: A shallow metal pan used to roast green coffee beans over an open flame.
  • Mukecha & Zenezena: A wooden mortar and pestle used to grind the freshly roasted beans by hand.
  • Sini: Small, handleless porcelain cups arranged closely together on a wooden tray.
